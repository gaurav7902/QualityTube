01 / The point

For people who would rather spend data than pixels.

Unlimited plan? Generous data cap? QualityTube is built for the simple preference: give me the best picture YouTube can offer, every time.

01

Highest available, automatically

It skips Auto, ignores disabled tiers, and works upward until YouTube confirms the best eligible quality.

Quiet by design

Your viewing stays yours.
